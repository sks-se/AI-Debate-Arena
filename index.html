Chapter One

The Awakening

You wake on cold stone. No memory of how you arrived. Above you, ancient columns rise into a sky that flickers like a broken signal — half temple, half machine.

A voice fills the chamber, calm and vast: "You have been chosen. The greatest minds in history have been recreated inside this Arena."

Each chamber holds one legendary thinker, pulled from history and rebuilt in light and logic. To move forward, you must face them — not with weapons, but with argument. Wit against wit. Reason against reason.

Win, and the Arena opens a path deeper toward the surface. Lose, and a fragment of who you are is taken and folded into the walls, added to the thousands who came before you.

⚠ LOSE THREE DEBATES, AND YOUR MEMORY BECOMES PART OF THE ARENA. FOREVER.
